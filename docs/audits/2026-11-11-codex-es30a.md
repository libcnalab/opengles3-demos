## Findings

### `es30-depth-textures`

- **WEAK —** [main.cpp](demos/es30/depth-textures/main.cpp:1987), also line 2004. The `expect_or_known_deviation` calls match the substring `"Vulkan"`, which can classify any Vulkan-backed implementation as the known deviation. The recorded evidence is specifically lavapipe (through zink and ANGLE on lavapipe); this is broader than the evidence and can turn a real failure on another Vulkan backend into XFAIL. The README’s deviation entry at lines 205–206 also mentions zink, while `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md` describes lavapipe through both zink and ANGLE.
  - **Spec:** ES 3.0 §3.9.2.1 says that for an incomplete texture, “0 will be returned for a shadow sampler.”
  - **Fix:** match the demonstrated lavapipe renderer/backend specifically (with distinct guards for zink and ANGLE if needed), and make the README’s driver scope match the guard. Keep the strict expectation for other backends.
